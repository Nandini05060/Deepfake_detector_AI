## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

Earlier experiments with LX-DFD revealed an alarming operational vulnerability: **the exact same synthetic image generated by Google Gemini (Imagen 3) produced diametrically opposed predictions under different valid preprocessing crop strategies**.

* Under a tight face-only crop (margin = 0.15), the model predicted **AUTHENTIC REAL with 99.85% confidence** ($P(\text{Fake}) = 0.0015$).
* Under a standard portrait crop (margin = 0.50), the model predicted **SYNTHETIC FAKE** ($P(\text{Fake}) = 0.5323$, rising to $0.7211$ in Phase 10).
* Under an unconstrained center-square crop, the model predicted **SYNTHETIC FAKE with 80.46% confidence**.

This study was launched to scientifically audit the relationship between face crop geometry and classification stability. **A robust forensic classifier must not rely on arbitrary cropping margins to render a verdict.**

---

## 2. Crop Strategy Definitions & Geometrical Rationale

Given an image of dimensions $W \times H$ and an OpenCV YuNet detected bounding box $[f_x, f_y, f_w, f_h]$:

1. **Strategy 1: Tight Face-Only Crop (Margin = 0.15)**
   $$\Delta x = 0.15 \cdot f_w, \quad \Delta y = 0.15 \cdot f_h$$
   Isolates internal facial landmarks (eyes, nose, mouth) while discarding the forehead hairline, ears, chin boundary, and collar.
2. **Strategy 2: Standard Portrait Crop (Margin = 0.50 — Selected Production Default)**
   $$\Delta x = 0.50 \cdot f_w, \quad \Delta y = 0.50 \cdot f_h$$
   Preserves the complete head silhouette, ears, hairline, jawline, and natural depth-of-field transition to the immediate background.
3. **Strategy 3: Extended-Context Crop (Margin = 0.75)**
   $$\Delta x = 0.75 \cdot f_w, \quad \Delta y = 0.75 \cdot f_h$$
   Captures broader upper-body context, clothing textures, and surrounding photographic environment.
4. **Strategy 4: Center-Square Crop (Fallback Baseline)**
   A centered square with side length $\min(W, H)$ without face localization. Used as a baseline to quantify the effect of unguided background inclusion.

## 4. Root Cause Analysis: Why Diffusion Detection Fails Under Tight Cropping

### The Physical & Algorithmic Mechanism
1. **Internal Photorealism:** Modern generative diffusion models (Google Gemini Imagen 3, Midjourney v6, SDXL) excel at synthesizing internal biometric morphology. Their facial skin pore generation, iris specular reflections, and teeth structures contain very few anatomical errors.
2. **Peripheral Blending Seams:** Generative latent diffusion models exhibit their highest error rates at **compositional boundaries**—the boundary where synthetic hair strands meet organic backgrounds, where earlobes dissolve into bokeh, and where lighting direction on the jawline diverges from ambient scene lighting.
3. **The Tight Crop Blunder:** Setting margin = 0.15 acts as an artificial low-pass anatomical filter, surgically excising the exact boundary pixels where diffusion models fail, leaving only the hyper-realistic center. The model naturally predicts Real.
4. **Context Margin Recovery:** Expanding the margin to 0.50 restores the hair boundary and peripheral depth transition into the $224 \times 224$ receptive field, doubling Stable Diffusion recall (from 26% to 51%) and correctly flipping Gemini Imagen 3 from 0.0015 to 0.5323 Fake probability.

## 5. Production Decision & The Crop Stability Lab

### 5.1 Selection of Margin 0.50 for Production
Based strictly on the validation data in Section 3:
* **Margin 0.50 balances high real specificity (98.0%) with maximum diffusion recall (51.0%)** without the catastrophic false alarm penalty of Center Crop (15.33% FPR).
* The final test set was left untouched during this optimization.

### 5.2 Implementation of the Crop Stability Lab
Because no single crop margin is universally optimal across all real-world capture resolutions, LX-DFD implemented the **Crop Stability Lab (Module 02)**:
* Simultaneously computes model predictions across margins 0.15, 0.50, and 0.75.
* Calculates the probability delta: $\Delta = \max(P) - \min(P)$.
* Categorizes stability:
  * **Low Variation ($\Delta \le 0.15$):** Biometrically robust decision.
  * **Moderate Variation ($0.15 < \Delta \le 0.35$):** Mild sensitivity to boundary context.
  * **High Variation ($\Delta > 0.35$):** Preprocessing-sensitive verdict.

### 5.3 Mandatory Forensic Disclaimer
As integrated into the live UI and forensic reports:
